The one act on a card (primary), the act beside it (ghost), a rare heavy act (ink), an inline act (link) or a row's verbs (size="sm").

```jsx
<Button variant="primary">Set password and enter</Button>
<Button variant="ghost">sign out</Button>
<Button variant="ghost" size="sm">re-run</Button>
<Button variant="link">→ open Larkspur Crossing</Button>
```

- Never two primaries in one card. Disabled renders at 45% opacity ("sending…").
- Words are lowercase verbs except the one primary sentence-case act.
